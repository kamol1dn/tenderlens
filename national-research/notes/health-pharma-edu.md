**Key:** "opened = yes" means the page was fetched with WebFetch and the number was seen there. "opened = no" means the number comes from a search snippet only. Treat "no" numbers as leads to verify before they go on a slide.

**Caveats that apply to all sections:**

## Candidate 1: Pharmacy price and compliance co-pilot (reference prices, marking, e-Rx, reimbursement)

### 1. Problem
About 12,000 licensed pharmacies and branches, mostly private SMBs, must comply with four overlapping digital regimes:
- reference-price ceilings that are revised in waves (1,970 drugs in Dec 2025, about 2,000 in Mar 2026, about 2,500 in Jul 2026);
- the "Asl belgisi" Data Matrix marking scheme, with revenue-based fines from 1 Jan 2026;
- e-prescriptions for antibiotics and hormonal drugs;
- the GPP certificate.

In practice, hundreds of thousands of price violations are found, and owners pay fines or lose licences.

- **SMB users:** pharmacy owners and pharmacists. They need to know, per SKU and on the day, the legal ceiling price and whether an e-Rx ID is required. Usually they work from messy supplier invoices with trade names: there are 82 imported versions of levofloxacin alone.
- **Government users and their gains:**
  - **Agency for Development of the Pharmaceutical Industry** (price registration and reference pricing desk): earlier compliance and less manual complaint handling.
  - **Competition Promotion and Consumer Protection Committee** (price inspectors): risk-ranked list of pharmacies to inspect.
  - **Center for Pharmaceutical Product Safety** (licensing): automatic referrals of systematic violators.
  - **Tax Committee** (marking and fiscal receipts): fewer marking violations.

### 3. What already exists (judges will ask)
- **pharm.gov.uz (now redirects to mpa.uz, "Medpharm portal").** Publishes reference prices and the registry. WebFetch got only a JavaScript shell, so it is not scrapable with a simple fetch.
- **PharmUz mobile app.** Drug search, reference prices, nearby pharmacies, complaints. The App Store lists the publisher only as "PharmUz", so official status is unverified (https://apps.apple.com/app/id6738636506).
- **Asl belgisi app.** Consumers scan the Data Matrix to check legality (https://yuz.uz/ru/news/kak-v-uzbekistane-mojno-proverit-legalnost-lekarstvennx-sredstv). The operator is CRPT Turon.
- **Fair Tech "single information system"** at the Competition Committee, plus hotline 1159.
  - spot.uz (5 Feb 2026, opened yes) says only "Через единую информационную систему Fair Tech поступила информация о превышении установленных референтных цен ... в 267 456 случаях".
  - **Unverified which of two readings is true:**
    - (a) A consumer complaint channel. Kun.uz words it this way.
    - (b) Automated monitoring of receipts or prices against ceilings. 267k-686k cases look too many to be complaints.
  - **If (b) is true,** the government-side risk ranking in this candidate already exists. The pitch must then lean on the pharmacy-side tool, and the both-sides benefit drops. Check this tonight before pitching.
- **Tax Committee online cash registers.** They already refuse a receipt without an e-Rx ID for reimbursed drugs, and block expired marked goods from 1 Mar 2026.
- **Private apps:** Apteka 1069, ArzonApteka, Go Pharm, Oson Apteka. They do consumer search and delivery, not compliance.
- **Gap:** none of these is a **pharmacy-side compliance tool**. Nothing takes the pharmacy's own supplier invoice or stock list and tells the owner what is non-compliant today, when the price list changed last week. The state tools are consumer-facing and punitive.

### 4. Data a team could collect tonight
- **Reference prices and register on pharm.gov.uz / mpa.uz.** JavaScript-rendered. Open it in a real browser and look for an XHR/JSON API or Excel export (not verified). Languages: Uzbek and Russian.
- **State register via uzpharm-control.uz.** A third-party analysis counts 6,572 registered medical product records (Version N30, 15 Jun 2026) (https://meddeviceguide.com/blog/uzbekistan-medical-device-registry-analysis, opened yes). Whether it can be downloaded is not confirmed.
- **Fallback if the portal cannot be scraped:** hand-type 50-100 reference prices from PharmUz or pharm.gov.uz for common drugs (antibiotics, cardio). Photograph 5-10 real pharmacy receipts or supplier invoices.
- **Legal texts on lex.uz.** Markup caps, PP-190 marking fines, e-Rx order. Can be fetched as HTML.

### 5. Foreign comparators
- **Turkey, ITS (Ilaç Takip Sistemi).** Point-of-dispense check from 2010, full track and trace from 2012; reimbursement depends on verified dispensing. A peer-reviewed study lists "elimination of reimbursement fraud and prevention of falsified medicine" as the main drivers (https://www.ghspjournal.org/node/3663, snippet, opened no). Savings of "$1 billion annually" is a vendor claim only (https://www.tigahealth.com/turkiye-saving-1-billion-dollars-annually-with-its, opened no).
- **Estonia, e-prescription.** About 80% of prescriptions were electronic 15 months after the 2010 launch, and 99% now (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8118601, snippet, opened no).

### 6. AI vs rules
- **Rules (no AI):** checking price against the ceiling, checking the markup cap, the "needs e-Rx?" flag, and the expiry check. All of these are lookups.
- **Genuine AI:**
  - Matching messy trade names, doses and pack sizes from photographed invoices or receipts (Uzbek/Russian/Latin/Cyrillic) to the registry entry. This is the "82 levofloxacins" problem: OCR plus LLM, or embedding-based entity matching.
  - LLM summaries of each new price wave or legal change in plain Uzbek ("these 37 SKUs in your stock changed ceiling today").
  - Government side: anomaly ranking of pharmacies from receipt and price patterns, to prioritise inspections.
- **Be honest with judges:** the core check is a lookup. The AI is the input normalisation.

### 7. Scores (1-5)
- **Pain evidence:** 5 (686k cases, 2,500 pharmacies, fines up to 2% of revenue, GPP fees; opened sources)
- **Benefit to both sides:** 4, or 3 if Fair Tech turns out to be automated monitoring (honest SMBs avoid fines; state inspects smarter. But most numbers frame SMBs as violators)
- **Track fit:** 4 (pharma plus entrepreneurship)
- **48h demo feasibility:** 3 (data access risk: the portal is JavaScript-only. OCR and matching are doable)
- **Strongest objection:** "Overpricing is deliberate, not confusion. PharmUz and pharm.gov.uz already show the ceiling price, and the Committee's Fair Tech system already flags violations. Why would a cheating pharmacy use your tool, and why does the state need it?"
  - **Counter:** sell it to *honest* owners and chains as fine insurance: invoice-to-registry matching plus alerts on each price wave. Offer risk scoring to the Committee only if Fair Tech is not already automated.

## Candidate 2: Kindergarten subsidy integrity and faster payment for private preschools (education + entrepreneurship)

### 1. Problem
The state pays per-child subsidies to private, family and public-private-partnership (PPP) kindergartens based on attendance logged in an online system. The system is gamed: fictitious and even deceased children are listed, and whole fictitious kindergartens exist. At the same time, honest operators can go unpaid for months. In the one documented case (131m soums), the article does not state why payment was withheld; it cites the regulation's clause 32 on geolocation, and payment came only after the Business Ombudsman stepped in.

- **SMB users:** owners of about 29,000 family kindergartens and other non-state kindergartens. They want fast, dispute-free subsidy payments and simple attendance reporting.
- **Government users and their gains:**
  - **Ministry of Preschool and School Education** (subsidy and finance department; NMTTBAT / "Bolalar bog'chasi" information system owners): targets ghost children before payment, not after a court case.
  - **District preschool education departments under khokimiyats:** a verification workload ranked by risk.
  - **Prosecutor General's department / Inspection for Education Quality Control:** audit leads.
  - **Business Ombudsman:** fewer complaints about unpaid subsidies.

### 3. What already exists
- **NMTTBAT, the non-state preschool information system.** The Ministry's system: attendance app, PPP e-contracts, and a geolocation check on attendance.
  - A merger into the "Bolalar bog'chasi" system was ordered with a 1 Feb 2025 deadline, but completion is not verified. The Dec 2025 article still names NMTTBAT.
- **Rule-based checks already exist** (geolocation, attendance-based pay), but fraud still passed for three years in the Khorezm case.
- **Gap:** no evidence of cross-registry analytics (deceased/civil registry, duplicate children across kindergartens, the same parent phone across many kindergartens, implausibly perfect attendance), and no appeal/explanation workflow for owners whose payment is blocked.
- **Kazakhstan tried Face ID in kindergartens.** The ministry admitted violations in the pilot and business (Atameken) protested (https://www.zakon.kz/obshestvo/6452337-v-detsadakh-kazakhstana-vyyavleny-narusheniya-pri-vnedrenii-Face-ID.html, snippet, opened no). This is a warning against a child-biometrics pitch.

- Language: Uzbek (Cyrillic and Latin) and Russian.

### 5. Foreign comparators
- **Kazakhstan, Supreme Audit Chamber, 2025 budget report.**
  - Private schools under per-capita state order got 149.2 bn tenge for >203k pupils in 559 schools.
  - Spending rose 20x in five years while real pupils rose about 6x.
  - "1333 учащихся переводились из одной школы в другую от 5 до 12 раз" (https://bes.media/amp/milliardi-na-myortvie-dushi-pochemu-chastnie-shkoli-bolshe-pro-biznes-d31edf/, 12 Jun 2026, opened yes).
  - This is exactly the duplicate-child pattern an anomaly model catches.
- **Kazakhstan, kindergartens.** Per-capita kindergarten financing since 2019 covers >5,000 kindergartens; attendance logging was moved to kindergartens to fight "dead souls" (https://kazpravda.kz/n/umnoe-finansirovanie-detsadov/, snippet, opened no).

### 6. AI vs rules
- **Rules:** child in two kindergartens on the same day; child flagged deceased in the civil registry; geolocation outside the PPP address.
- **Genuine AI:**
  - Unsupervised anomaly scoring of attendance time series: too-perfect attendance, mass check-ins in seconds, identical patterns across kindergartens, sudden enrolment spikes before subsidy months.
  - Graph analysis linking owners, phones, addresses and officials.
  - LLM-generated explanation and appeal letters for owners. Each flag is explained in Uzbek with the documents to upload, which shortens disputes like the 131m case.
  - Optional: vision check of group photos for headcount (count only, not identity, to avoid the biometrics backlash).

### 7. Scores
- **Pain evidence:** 4 (court cases, 1.3 bn and 161m frauds, 131m unpaid; no national fraud rate found)
- **Benefit to both sides:** 4 (the state stops leakage on a 4.7-trillion line. Honest SMBs get paid faster and with fewer disputes, but the SMB-side pain rests on one documented case)
- **Track fit:** 5 (education plus entrepreneurship)
- **48h demo feasibility:** 4 (synthetic data plus a simple anomaly model plus an LLM explainer is very doable)
- **Strongest objection:** "The Ministry's Bolalar bog'chasi system already does attendance and geolocation, and the Namangan case involved officials, so analytics won't stop collusion. Where does your real data come from?"
  - **Counter:** position it as a risk-scoring layer and an appeals assistant on top of the existing system. A pilot needs one district's export.

## Candidate 3: e-Prescription dispensing gap and OTC antibiotic sales

### 1. Problem
Since 16 Dec 2025, antibiotics and systemic hormones may be dispensed only against an e-prescription (Tashkent plus 15 pilot districts; nationwide planned by end-2026). Yet only a fraction of e-prescriptions show up as dispensed in pharmacies, and antibiotic misuse is high. The administrative fine for "illegal sale of prescription drugs" is now UZS 41-82m. Not confirmed: whether this article applies to an ordinary antibiotic sale without an e-Rx.

- **SMB users:** pharmacists, who need to verify an e-Rx quickly. Private clinics also issue e-prescriptions (25,393 so far).
- **Government users and their gains:**
  - **Agency for Development of the Pharmaceutical Industry** (e-Rx programme team): sees where the gap is.
  - **Tax Committee** (fiscal-data analysts): a targeted list of pharmacies to check.
  - **Ministry of Health** (antimicrobial resistance / AMR focal point): AMR monitoring.

## Candidate 4: Claims and ICD-10/DRG coding assistant for private clinics entering State Health Insurance (low-evidence but government-requested)

### 1. Problem
From 1 Jul 2026, licensed private clinics can treat state-funded patients under the State Health Insurance Fund. SHI covers the whole country from 1 Oct 2026, and from 2028 the Fund buys only from accredited providers. Private clinics (SMBs) must code diagnoses and procedures (ICD-10, DRG case-mix) and submit claims. SHIF itself names "coding support, claim review" and "detect upcoding" as AI priorities.

- **SMB users:** private clinic administrators and coders.
- **Government users and their gains:** **SHIF** claims adjudication and the MED Express / DRG grouper team. They gain cleaner claims and fraud flags.
