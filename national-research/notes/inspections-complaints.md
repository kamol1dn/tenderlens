## 0. Key reform facts (read this first: judges will know them)

| Reform | What it does | Source | opened |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PF-175, 27 Aug 2026** (in force 28 Aug 2026), "On measures to implement the tasks of the VI open dialogue with entrepreneurs" | 3-year moratorium on **any** inspection of **small** businesses. Exceptions: criminal cases, impact on human health, labour law, citizens' appeals, VAT refunds, liquidation. Inspecting during the moratorium is an offence. The Business Ombudsman runs "Samarali nazorat": a review of control bodies' inspection files from the **last 3 years**, including on-site visits. From **1 Jan 2027**, any repeat inspection within one year needs the **Ombudsman's permission** (desk tax audits are exempt). "First violation = warning": no fine and **10 days to fix** if there was no harm to life, health or property. Voluntary **profilaktik (preventive) audit** for medium and large firms. Preventive audits also planned in fire, construction, sanitary and quarantine oversight (item 56). | **PF-63, 17 Apr 2026** "On limiting unlawful interference in business and increasing transparency of control measures" | From 1 Jul 2026, an updated **"Yagona davlat nazorati" (Unified State Control)** system and mobile app: the business sees the inspector's details, can chat with and rate them, and gets alerts about attempts by people without authority. From 1 Aug 2026, paper inspection journals and inspector ID cards are abolished. The inspector must scan the business's QR code ("Biznes himoya"), and if the visit is not in the system the business may refuse entry. An inspector who visits the same business twice in a year without grounds is barred from it for 12 months. | https://anhor.uz/news/business-oversight/ ; https://www.spot.uz/ru/2026/07/30/biznes-himoya/ (snippet) | yes / no |

Verbatim (PF-175, para 1): "Kichik tadbirkorlik subyektlarining faoliyatini har qanday tekshirishga uch yil muddatga moratoriy eʼlon qilinsin"

**What burden survives the moratorium.** A judge will say "PF-175 already fixed inspections", so every candidate has to answer this.
1. **Medium and large businesses** are not covered.
2. The exceptions stay open for small businesses: **health-impact** checks (sanitary/SES, pharma), **labour**, **citizens' complaints**, **VAT refund**.
3. **Letters and notifications with 10-day ultimatums** from many agencies are not "inspections". Entrepreneurs complain about them (zamin.uz, below).
4. The first-violation warning still demands a **fix within 10 days**.
5. The **Ombudsman's workload goes up**: a 3-year file review, plus permits for every repeat inspection from 1 Jan 2027.
6. Desk (kameral) tax audits continue.
7. Post-moratorium inspection counts (Sep-Nov 2026): **no number found**. One targeted check was made: the spot.uz biznes-ombudsman tag page had nothing on inspection counts between 2 Sep and 10 Oct 2026. The only post-reform data point is 4,254 regulator-business interactions registered in govcontrol since 1 Aug 2026 (uzdaily, 10 Oct 2026; covers the PF-63 period, not the moratorium period).
8. Open question: whether routine SES, pharmacy and fire checks count as "studying the impact on human health" (the exception's wording) is **not confirmed** by any source opened.

## Candidate 1: Inspection legality checker plus repeat-inspection permit triage for the Business Ombudsman (with an SMB "is this inspection legal?" bot)

**Problem (one sentence).** Inspection bodies run tens of thousands of checks a year; in 2025 the Ombudsman found 9,994 inspection violations, 567 of them wholly unlawful inspections. From 2027 the Ombudsman must also approve every repeat inspection and audit 3 years of inspection files, while small business owners cannot tell whether an inspector at the door (or a 10-day letter) is lawful under PF-175, PF-63 and the exceptions.

Sizing caveat: the 2027 permit workload is **unknown**. The 6,350 repeat-inspected businesses in H1 2026 do not size it, because desk tax audits are exempt and the Tax Committee accounted for 72% of those repeats.

- **SMB users:** small shops, cafes, pharmacies and service firms that receive an inspector or a demand letter. Medium firms still subject to inspections.
- **Government user:** **Business Ombudsman office** (central and regional staff: inspection-monitoring lawyers). They gain triage of permit requests (lawful ground? repeat in 12 months? exception category?), a pre-screened list of suspect inspection files for "Samarali nazorat", and auto-drafted representations and protests.
- **Secondary government user:** inspection bodies' legal units (**Tax Committee, Ministry of Emergency Situations (fire), SES/Sanitary-Epidemiological Committee, labour inspection**), which can self-check an order before issuing it.

### What already exists ("doesn't X already do this?")
- **govcontrol.uz / "Yagona davlat nazorati" app** (PF-63): it registers each visit by QR code, shows the inspector's identity, and lets the business rate and chat. It is a **record of the visit**. It does not tell the owner whether the **legal ground** is valid under PF-175's exceptions, and it gives the Ombudsman no legal analysis. The site is a JS single-page app, and curl returned only a shell ("GovControl.uz — Ягона давлат назорати ахборот тизими").
- **Business Ombudsman "Tadbirkor virtual ofisi"** (Entrepreneur Virtual Office): 7,507 appeals in 2025 (uza link above). This is complaint intake, not legality analysis.
- **LexAI** (Ministry of Justice; ai.lex.uz is linked from the lex.uz header): a legal Q&A chatbot according to a Dec 2024 report (https://www.spot.uz/oz/2024/12/26/lex-ai/, snippet, opened no). Its current features were **not checked**: the curl of ai.lex.uz failed.
- **PF-175 table item 13** plans government "AI assistants" for construction, labour, foreign trade and public services, with a pilot only in **June 2027**. A hackathon demo fits as a prototype for this mandate; it does not compete with a live product.
- **MoJ + KOICA "AI-based legal service" project** (2026-2030, USD 14.5M): a cooperation protocol reported by uza on 6 May 2026 (article date; the signing date is not confirmed). Still planned, not live. https://uza.uz/ar/posts/uzbekistan-launches-ai-based-legal-services_854281 (opened yes; quote: "introducing artificial intelligence technologies into the sector is strategically important.")

### AI vs rules
- **Rules:** is this a small business (registry)? Is the ground in the PF-175 exception list? Was there an inspection by the same body within 12 months? Is the visit in govcontrol? These are simple lookups and a decision tree.
- **AI needed:**
  1. Read a scanned or photographed **inspection order or demand letter** (OCR plus LLM extraction of body, legal ground, period, inspector, deadline) in Uzbek Latin, Uzbek Cyrillic or Russian.
  2. Map free-text grounds to exception categories (classification).
  3. Explain the result to the owner in plain Russian or Uzbek.
  4. Draft the complaint to the Ombudsman, or the Ombudsman's permit decision or representation.
  5. Cluster thousands of file reviews to flag anomalies, e.g. "same inspector, same business, different pretexts".

### Scores (1-5)
- Pain evidence: **4** (strong 2025-2026 numbers, but pre-moratorium; post-moratorium volume unknown)

- 48h demo feasibility: **4** (rules are public; OCR plus LLM plus a decision tree; synthetic orders)

Answer: medium firms; the exceptions (health, labour, complaints, VAT); 10-day letters; the new permit and 3-year review workload on the Ombudsman. But no post-September number is available.

## Candidate 2: Self-check (preventive) compliance copilot for health-related SMBs (pharmacies, private clinics, food outlets): bridge to the pharma & healthcare track

**Problem (one sentence).** Sanitary/SES and pharmaceutical-safety inspections were **rising** in H1 2026. They **may** stay outside the moratorium: the exception covers inspections "related to studying the impact on human health", and whether routine SES, pharmacy or fire checks fall under it is an **open question, not confirmed**. PF-175 item 56 plans voluntary "profilaktik" (preventive) audits, including in sanitary and fire oversight (a planned measure, not yet in force), and the decree gives first violations a 10-day fix window. Yet pharmacies, clinics and cafes have no plain-language, activity-specific self-check list derived from the scattered sanitary rules (SanPiN), pharma rules and fire rules, which exist mainly in Uzbek.

- **SMB users:** pharmacies, private clinics and dental offices, cafes and food producers.
- **Government users:** the **Sanitary-Epidemiological Welfare and Public Health Committee** (inspectors and prevention units), the **Pharmaceutical Products Safety Center** (Ministry of Health), and the **Ministry of Emergency Situations** (fire prevention). They gain fewer violations found at inspection, a standard checklist that supports the planned preventive audit, and data on which requirements are most often failed.

## Candidate 3: Appeals triage and systemic-issue detection for the Business Ombudsman (and the business slice of People's Reception appeals)

**Problem (one sentence).** The Business Ombudsman received 9,555 appeals in 2025 and already 10,743 in Jan-Sep 2026, with only about 48-49% resolved positively. Presidential People's Receptions registered 76,196 appeals in August 2026 alone, 11,114 of them repeats. How much routing and answering is automated is **unknown** (PF-51 mandates categorisation and auto-escalation but does not say how).

- **SMB users:** entrepreneurs filing complaints (tax, land/cadastre, officials' decisions, inspections, bank credit).
- **Government users:** **Business Ombudsman office** appeal handlers, and **Presidential People's Reception** staff (new 49-person central office under PF-51) and hokims' receptions. They gain auto-classification by topic and agency, duplicate and repeat detection, draft replies, and a "systemic issue" dashboard (PF-51 requires working groups on systemic problems, reporting quarterly).

## Candidate 4: "What changed for my business": a regulatory-change radar in Russian and Uzbek, plus draft-impact screening for the Ombudsman and the Ministry of Justice

**Problem (one sentence).** Hundreds of new acts a year (14,544 acts in force on lex.uz in mid-2025, of which 541 were adopted in H1 2025 alone) plus 293 drafts under discussion on 17 Nov 2026 are published officially in Uzbek, with Russian translations marked unofficial. Small and medium businesses learn about new obligations too late, while the Ombudsman reviews hundreds of drafts a year by hand and officials adopted 7,000+ unlawful normative documents in 2025.

- **SMB users:** owners and accountants, often Russian-speaking.
- **Government users:** **Ministry of Justice** (lex.uz / regulation.gov.uz operators; legal-expertise department) and **Business Ombudsman** draft reviewers (898 drafts reviewed in 2025 with 780 objections; 349 in H1 2026; 800+ in Jan-Sep 2026). They gain auto-screening of drafts for new burdens on business, and better-informed businesses mean fewer violations.

### What already exists
- **lex.uz**: has Russian (unofficial) and English versions, an "ONLINE TRANSLATE" link, **ai.lex.uz** (LexAI, a Justice Ministry AI Q&A chatbot per a Dec 2024 report; snippet only, current features unchecked), **sharh.lex.uz** (commentaries) and the **@huquqiyaxborot** Telegram channel. All were seen as links in the lex.uz page header via curl.
- **regulation.gov.uz**: public draft discussion with categories and a "Statistika" section.
- **norma.uz / buxgalter.uz**: paid Russian-language commentaries for accountants. They are commercial competitors.
- Gap: no free **push** service along the lines of "this new act affects activity code X, here is what to do by date Y". Also no auto-screening of drafts for business burden. Not verified as absent: **verify with mentors**.

### Public data tonight
- **lex.uz**: search results are HTML, filterable by year (2026, 2025 ...). A test search returned "28540 hujjat topildi", so it is scrapable with a browser user agent. Document pages are in uz/ru/en.
- **regulation.gov.uz**: homepage counters and categories in HTML; draft pages are scrapable (to be tested). Uzbek and Russian.
- **Business Ombudsman** objections are reported in news only; no dataset.

### AI vs rules
- **Rules:** new-document feed, filters by issuing body and date.
- **AI needed:**
  1. Summarise a new act into "who is affected / what changes / from when / penalty".
  2. Map it to activity codes (OKED) or business profiles (classification).
  3. Uzbek to Russian plain-language explanation.
  4. Diff between the old and new versions of an act.
  5. Screen drafts for new burdens (new permits, fees, reporting duties, deadlines) for Ombudsman reviewers.

## Overall ranking (this theme)
1. **Candidate 1: Inspection legality checker plus Ombudsman permit/file-review triage.** Strongest evidence, and it fits the brand-new 2027 duties PF-175 puts on the Ombudsman. The PF-175 "AI assistants" pilot is not due until June 2027.
2. **Candidate 2: Preventive self-check copilot for pharmacies and clinics.** Best track fit (pharma & healthcare): SES and pharma inspections were rising in H1 2026 and may fall under the "human health" exception, which **needs confirmation**. Pain numbers are thin.
3. Candidate 4 (regulatory radar): feasible, but LexAI overlaps.
4. Candidate 3 (appeals triage): PF-51 overlap, and People's Reception appeals are mostly social, not business.

A possible merge: candidates 1 and 2 as one product. "Inspection shield" for a pharmacy or cafe owner: (a) self-check before inspection; (b) "is this inspection or letter lawful?"; (c) auto-complaint to the Business Ombudsman; plus (d) an Ombudsman-side dashboard for triage of repeat-inspection permits.

## Gaps and caveats
- 65,737 / +26.5% (Jan-Apr 2026) rests on one outlet (zamin.uz) citing unnamed "official statistics". The primary release was not found.
- 148,000+ (2025) is inspections "coordinated" through the Ombudsman, not a national total.
- People's Reception figures differ in scope across uza articles: 76k in August vs 156k in Jan-Sep. Do not combine them.
- kun.uz H1 2026 recovery figure: 13.3bn (body) vs 133bn (headline), unresolved.
- No post-moratorium (Sep-Nov 2026) inspection counts were found. One targeted check (the spot.uz Ombudsman tag page, Sep to 10 Oct 2026) turned up nothing. **All inspection volumes above are from before the moratorium.**
- Whether routine SES, pharmacy and fire checks fall under the "impact on human health" exception is unconfirmed. This is key for Candidate 2.
- The 6,000 / 15,000 / 55% / 12,448 rows are snippet-only with no identified URL. Do not use them in a pitch.
- daryo.uz, eimin.lrv.lt and some gov sites return 403 to WebFetch. daryo was opened via curl; eimin sits behind a Cloudflare challenge and was not opened.
