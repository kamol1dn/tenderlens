## Candidate A: AI pre-check and refusal triage for business-facing state services (Cadastre / land and premises first)

### 1. Problem
SMBs that register property rights, lease rights, cadastre passports, or other land and premises services lose weeks to deadline breaches and "unjustified refusals". The **Cadastre Agency is the largest single agency by violations: 19,233 of 112,208**. These counts cover **all applicants, citizens included**. No business-only split was found, and refusals have no per-agency split. An AI pre-check of the application package, with a plain-language "why was I refused, and what do I fix" explainer, cuts resubmissions.
- **SMB users:** small business owners and individual entrepreneurs who rent or register premises (shops, cafés, workshops), and accountants and lawyers who file on their behalf.
- **Gov users:** **Ministry of Justice, Public Services Agency** (the monitoring and control department that counts violations: it gets automatic classification of refusals and complaints by reason and agency). **Cadastre Agency** (regional registrars: fewer incomplete packages and fewer repeat visits). **Khokimiyats** (land-allocation decisions feed into these services).

## Candidate B: Hiring and formalisation copilot (SMB) with risk triage for the State Labour Inspection

### 1. Problem
Small employers (cafés, construction subcontractors, workshops, shops) do not know how to hire legally and cheaply. They are unsure which status to use (employee on an e-contract in my.mehnat.uz, self-employed, the 1% social-tax low-wage scheme) and what the Labour Code requires. So they stay informal. The presidential decree of 4 Aug 2025 *provides for* a fine of 30 BCU, UZS 12.36M as of Nov 2025. Whether it is in force, and the current amount, still need checking on lex.uz.
- **SMB users:** owners of micro and small firms with 1–20 staff, and their accountants.
- **Gov users:** **Ministry of Employment and Poverty Reduction**, specifically the **State Labour Inspection** (inspectors and the new desk/"kameral" control unit). They get pre-filled, compliant e-contracts and a risk-ranked list of employers for warning letters instead of random visits. The gain counts toward the presidential legalisation target. The Tax Committee gains a wider social-tax base.
- **SMB incentive (must be explicit in the pitch):** fine avoidance (UZS 12.36M per first offence as of Nov 2025, in-force status to verify) and fewer inspection triggers. A secondary, weaker incentive is the 1% social-tax rate for qualifying hires. It is snippet-only, and a Sept 2026 MoEF proposal would phase such incentives out, so don't build the pitch on it. Without an incentive the tool only raises the firm's costs.

## Data traps (do not put these on slides)
- "5.4 million": one source calls it informal workers (38%), another calls it formal workers (of 14.7M). Ambiguous, so don't use it.
- my.gov.uz "525 / 800 / 905" are **service types**, not deliveries.
- "76.5M services" has **no stated period**. Don't call it annual.
- The 538 bn demolition compensation covers **all** building types, not only businesses.
- The legalisation target is 350k in the headline but 250k (100k + 200k) in the body.
- The 45% skills-gap figure (Euronews) is unsourced.
- The 254 → 84 day permit target is undated. The 90–120 days figure is pre-reform (Feb 2026).
- The uza.uz violation counts (112,208 / 18.6k refusals / Cadastre 19,233) cover **all applicants, citizens included**, not just businesses. Refusals have no per-agency split.
- "39.4M my.gov.uz services Jan–Sep 2025" has no captured URL. Don't use it.
- The 12.36M-soum informal-employment fine: in-force status and current amount are not verified on lex.uz.
