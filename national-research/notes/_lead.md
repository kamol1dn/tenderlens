## Government AI agenda: framing for judges
- On 21 Oct 2025 (presidential videoconference), the government announced 100 sectoral AI projects in 2026: "sog'liqni saqlash, energetika, bank-moliya, bojxona-soliq, transport, tog'-kon va qishloq xo'jaligi". Also from 2026 an AI-adoption rating of ministries and regions, AI hackathons in all regions to find youth projects, and the Urgench smart-city system where AI analyses citizen appeals (claimed saving of 16 bn UZS a year). https://www.spot.uz/oz/2025/10/21/ai-projects/ (VERIFIED via WebFetch)
- Government Resolution No. 581 of 20 Oct 2026: free LLM API access for up to 6 months, 10M tokens/month, for startups and higher-education and scientific organisations. Selected by competition twice a year (July, November). https://anhor.uz/uzl/news/ai-infratuzilma-ijtimoiy-loyihalar (VERIFIED). Pitch hook: a post-hackathon path to run on state AI infrastructure.
